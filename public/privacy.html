Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how OBA Downloader handles information when you use this website. OBA Downloader helps process links to publicly accessible media from supported social platforms.

Information you submit

When you paste a link or copied share message, the link is sent to our server so we can identify the platform and request the media. Please do not submit private links, passwords, account credentials, or other sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

To process downloads, submitted links may be shared with the service provider used for that platform. TikTok links are processed through the TikWM service. Instagram, Facebook and other enabled platforms are processed through our configured Cobalt download engine. These providers and the relevant social platforms may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. OBA Downloader does not control their independent practices.

Technical information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information, such as IP address, request time, browser details and diagnostic logs, to deliver, secure and maintain the service. We do not currently require users to create an account to use the downloader, and this website does not intentionally ask for names, phone numbers or payment details.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use advertising cookies or a third-party analytics tool. Your browser may store the site's app resources through its service worker to help the interface load efficiently.

How information is used

Submitted links are used to process the download request, protect the service from misuse, and troubleshoot technical problems. Do not use the service to access or save private, restricted or unauthorised content.

Children's privacy

This service is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please avoid sharing it further and contact us through the OBA Digital Studio contact channels.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact OBA Digital Studio through its official website.
