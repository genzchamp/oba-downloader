How to download an Instagram video or Reel

  1. Open Instagram and find the public Reel or video post.
  2. Tap Share or the post menu, then choose Copy link.
  3. Open OBA Downloader and paste the copied Instagram link.
  4. Tap Download video. If your browser does not start the download, use the link shown in the result.

Before you download
