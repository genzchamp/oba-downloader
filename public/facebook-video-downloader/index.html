How to download a Facebook video or Reel

  1. Open Facebook and find the public video or Reel.
  2. Tap the post's Share option and choose Copy link.
  3. Visit OBA Downloader and paste the link into the input field.
  4. Tap Download video. If the download does not begin automatically, tap the download link shown in the result.

What should I know?

OBA Downloader supports tested public Facebook video and Reels links. Private posts, audience-restricted content, deleted posts or links requiring a login may not be available.

Only download content you are allowed to save. Respect the creator's rights and Facebook's terms, and ask permission before reposting someone else's work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download Facebook Reels?

Yes, supported public Facebook Reels links are included.

Why does my Facebook link not work?

Check that you copied the full link from the Share menu and that the post is public. Some links may be restricted or temporarily unavailable.

Do I need a Facebook password on OBA Downloader?

No. Do not enter your Facebook password on OBA Downloader. Paste only the public post or Reel link.

Can I use the video anywhere?

Not automatically. Downloading does not transfer copyright or grant permission to republish. Obtain permission where required.
