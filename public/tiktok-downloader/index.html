How to download a TikTok video

  1. Open TikTok and find the public video you want to save.
  2. Tap Share, then choose Copy link.
  3. Open OBA Downloader and paste the copied link or share message.
  4. Tap Download video. When the file is ready, use the download link if your browser does not start automatically.

Using TikTok Lite? Copy the share link from TikTok Lite and paste it the same way.

Helpful notes

OBA Downloader works with supported public TikTok video links. Private, restricted, removed or otherwise unavailable posts may not download. If a link fails, copy it again from the Share menu and retry.

Please respect the creator's copyright and the platform's terms. Only save or reuse content when you have permission or another lawful basis.

Frequently asked questions

Does it support TikTok Lite?

Yes. TikTok Lite share links are supported. Copy the link from the app and paste it into OBA Downloader.

Do I need to install an app?

No. OBA Downloader runs in your browser, including on mobile devices.

Why might a TikTok link fail?

The post may be private, restricted, deleted or temporarily unavailable. Try copying a fresh link to a public video.

Can I repost a downloaded video?

Downloading does not give you ownership. Get the creator's permission and follow applicable copyright rules before reposting or using the video.
